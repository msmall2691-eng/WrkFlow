A quote calculator for service businesses. Visitors price the job from your prices and send you the lead.

== Description ==

Visitors pick a service, set the size, tick any add-ons, and see a price range worked out from your own prices. Then they send their name and contact details, and the lead comes to you with the service, size and estimate attached.

* Your services, prices and add-ons, set in Settings > Instant Quote
* Your button color and wording
* Works in any theme. Styles are isolated so your theme cannot break it and it cannot break your theme
* Leads by email (with an M Studio site key), to your own form endpoint, or through the visitor's email app

== Installation ==

1. Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, and choose the zip.
2. Activate it.
3. Go to Settings > Instant Quote and enter your services and prices.
4. Add `[instant_quote]` to any page. In the block editor, use a Shortcode block.

Shortcode options: `[instant_quote title="Price your project"]` changes the heading on that page.
